The protein number most lifters need is lower than you think.

A 2018 meta-analysis (Morton et al., British Journal of Sports Medicine) pooled 49 studies and 1,863 people doing resistance training. Extra protein stopped adding muscle at around 1.6 g per kg of bodyweight per day.

Your number = bodyweight in kg × 1.6.
