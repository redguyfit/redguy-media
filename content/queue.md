# Content queue (in order)

Audience: men 18–40. Each post opens on a problem they know ("my chest won't grow", "skinny arms"…) and answers it with one study.
Focus: hypertrophy, chest, biceps, triceps, back. Rotate: body-part post, then general hypertrophy post. Every 4th post is a "Top 5 exercises" for one muscle.

Top 5 rules: hook = "Top 5 [muscle] exercises", then one exercise per slide (name + one-line why), CTA. 7 slides.
Each pick needs a reason from a source opened that run: a hypertrophy study is best; EMG studies must be labeled
"muscle activation", not growth. If an exercise has no study, the slide gives the mechanical reason (stretch, load, range)
and the caption says the ranking is our pick, not a study result. Never claim "the best" without a source.

Topics only. Each run must confirm the study and every number on a page it opened before writing. Sources listed are leads, not verified claims.
If a lead can't be confirmed, find another study on the same problem, or state that the evidence is limited.
