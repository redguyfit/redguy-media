# BlueBro inspiration notes

Henere's screenshots of BlueBro posts (15 Nov 2026). Take the idea, never the wording, drawings, character, layouts one-to-one or product names.

## What their grid shows
- Covers: big centered 2–3 line title, two colours, a small "(parenthetical)" line under it that adds a promise or a twist
  ("from worst to best", "more exercises ≠ more growth"). Mascot centered under the title, often cropped at the bottom.
- Recurring formats: tier lists / rankings, "rated out of 10", "X exercises I wish I started sooner", "popular exercises that
  waste your time (and what to do instead)", "muscles everyone forgets", "what every body fat % looks like", "best exercise for every muscle".
- On covers the mascot often does the exercise, or the target muscle is highlighted in a contrasting colour.

## Their viral post (8 Nov, ~10.6K likes, 6.2K shares): "how many exercises each muscle needs"
- 12 slides, one muscle per slide: muscle name + one huge number + one short line + small body diagram with the muscle highlighted
  + 1–4 labelled exercise illustrations. Very little text, very "save/share-able" (a reference sheet).
- Carousel posted WITH a trending song (added in the app). Same for their other carousels.
- Last slide: "Comment <keyword> and I'll DM you the link" (lead magnet). Caption = 2 short lines.

## How we use it (our own version)
- Covers: centered title + small parenthetical twist line; mascot centered and cropped (done from Post 004).
- Reference-sheet posts ("cheat sheets") with one muscle or one fix per slide and big visuals. Needs exercise poses → POSES-TODO priority.
- Muscle highlight in CYAN (our accent) on the red mascot, never their red-on-blue look.
- Add a trending sound to carousels too when Henere posts in the app.
- Comment-keyword CTA only if Henere has something to send and answers the DMs himself (we never reply as him).
