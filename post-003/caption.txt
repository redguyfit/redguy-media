Upper chest not growing? Tilt the bench.

In an 8-week study by Chaves et al. (International Journal of Exercise Science, 2020), untrained men did flat bench, 44° incline bench, or both. Only the incline group grew more at the upper chest; mid and lower chest changed about the same in all groups. Small study (10 men per group for muscle size), beginners, Smith machine only.

The rule: want more upper chest? Put incline pressing in your week. Flat still builds the rest.
