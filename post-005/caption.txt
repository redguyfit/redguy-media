That pinch in the front of your shoulder at the bottom of a bench rep? Check your setup before you quit pressing.

When the elbows flare out wide, the shoulders roll off the bench and the bar drifts toward your neck, the front of the shoulder takes the load your chest should. Small setup changes usually move it back where it belongs.

The rule: elbows tucked a bit, shoulder blades back and down, bar to the lower chest. Sharp or lasting pain → see a physio.
