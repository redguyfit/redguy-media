Light weights build muscle too.

A meta-analysis by Schoenfeld et al. (European Journal of Sport Science, 2016) compared light loads (60% of max or less) with heavy loads (65% or more): both built muscle. Heavy edged ahead, but the gap wasn't statistically significant, and most subjects were beginners.

The rule: light or heavy, both grow muscle. Keep some heavy work if you want max strength.
