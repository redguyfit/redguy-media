Want wider shoulders? Stop only pressing.

Shoulder width mostly comes from the side delts. Presses work the front of the shoulder hard, but the sides need their own move: the lateral raise, done light and done right.

The rule: lean a little forward, lift out to the sides with your elbows leading, stop at shoulder height, lower slowly. Twice a week.

Are lateral raises in your week right now? Yes or no 👇
